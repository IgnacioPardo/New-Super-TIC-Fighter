# Arena artwork production briefs

Both images are original game backdrops for the approved new environments. Preserve the supplied human fighter sprites. Scenery remains behind separate, clearly readable gameplay platforms. No damaging or moving environmental mechanics.

## Laboratorio TIC

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: landscape background, 1536×1024 or wider, cropped to 16:9 in-game.

A late-afternoon Argentine school computer lab, retro photographic/cutout aesthetic consistent with a 2017 indie platform fighter. Long rows of old beige and dark desktop monitors, tangled network cables, school desks, a worn green chalkboard with tiny illegible computing diagrams, fluorescent ceiling lights, broad windows with warm sunset light. Empty room, no people. Camera straight-on with modest depth, not an isometric game map. Rich photographic textures with restrained posterized/pixel-treated finish. Keep the centre of the image visually quiet and darker so 60×96 colourful human sprites remain readable. Muted teal, warm cream, copper sunlight. Environmental objects are background scenery, not prominent floating platforms. No readable text, logos, title, HUD, border, watermark or characters.

## Dentro de la PC

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: landscape background, 1536×1024 or wider, cropped to 16:9 in-game.

Inside an enormous retro desktop computer, a miniature-fighter viewpoint among oversized circuit boards, matte black integrated circuits, copper traces, capacitors, ribbon cables and a large cooling fan. Retro photographic/cutout aesthetic for a 2017-style indie platform fighter, realistic material textures with restrained posterized/pixel-treated finish. Camera straight-on, modest depth, clear darker central action space. Deep forest green circuit boards, copper/gold traces, cool cyan status lights and small amber highlights. Different silhouette and atmosphere from a school classroom. Empty environment, no people or characters. Background circuitry recedes; avoid high-contrast clutter in the centre. No text, logos, title, HUD, border or watermark. Fan is decorative only. No floating gameplay platforms baked into the image.
