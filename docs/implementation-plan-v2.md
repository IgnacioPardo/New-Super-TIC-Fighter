# New Super TIC Fighter — accepted v2 implementation plan

Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-11. This extends `implementation-plan.md`; its v1 history and compatibility requirements remain relevant. Implement the complete scope below. No unit tests, paid services, AI image generation, original-source mutation, or imported legacy user database.

## Gameplay and content

- Classic preserves the four v1 disciplines, moves, movement, and arena behavior. Expanded is the default for new games and offers ten disciplines. Preserve saved settings and bindings; all fighters are available immediately, progression is cosmetic only.
- Expanded adds double jump, forward/down aerial attacks, directional ground dodge/one air dash, upward recovery on the airborne signature button, fast-fall, and clearer guard feedback. Recovery/dash replenish on landing/respawn, without infinite-flight resets. Dodge defaults: gamepad L1/LB, keyboards Q/U/T/NumpadDecimal; flag saved-binding conflicts, never silently replace them.
- Author anticipation/contact/recovery poses and effects for new moves; do not just rotate the old jump frame. Keep the original layered fighter art, customization, soundtrack identity, and existing UI icons.
- Roster: Redes (cables), Diseño (mouse/selection zones), Programación (code/columns), Hardware (RAM/disc), IA (delayed own-attack echoes), Robótica (mechanical reach and one drone), IoT (ESP32/Arduino boards, sensors/wireless and Raspberry Pi hub; maximum two destructible boards), Realidad Virtual (displacement/one readable decoy), Videojuegos (ricochet projectile/bounce pad), Impresión 3D (piston and maximum two temporary destructible blocks). All deployables are bounded, deterministic, and cannot seal respawns or indefinitely trap fighters. IoT and Robótica must remain distinct.
- Preserve three arenas; add Embedded Workshop (benches/conveyor/press), Virtual Lab (telegraphed changing platforms), Arcade Circuit (larger Royale arena). Hazards/items are independent switches, both off by default. Classic stays static.
- Five items: throwable USB, cooling fan, firewall defense, battery ability restoration, temporary overclock; visible durations and simulation-owned effects.
- Modes: existing stock FFA/2v2 (3 stocks/5 minutes); timed KO (3 minutes, KOs minus self-destructs); Hill (3 minutes, uncontested zone control); best-of 1/3/5 sets (default 3); 8-bout Arcade with Hard final opponent; Survival for 1–2 humans couch/private-online and maximum four active fighters; 12 authored Challenges; Training damage/position reset, hitboxes, input history, frame advance, dummy recording/playback; Royale eight simultaneous fighters, one life, shrinking safe zone, four-minute limit, up to four local humans.
- Stock retains ties; timed/Hill use short sudden death; Royale records survival placement with shared placement for simultaneous eliminations. Camera follows local fighter or frames surviving local fighters; remote offscreen indicators and compact opponent roster. Hard bots use bounded reaction delays and all bots understand new arenas/objectives/recovery/deployables.

## Public online, accounts, competition

- Keep TS/React/Tailwind/Phaser; framework-independent simulation in a worker locally and on Cloudflare server. Worker static/API routing; hibernating Lobby Durable Object for presence/party/queue/reservations; Match DO owns public simulation and validated results; Tournament DO schedules/advances durable brackets/standings; D1/Drizzle stores accounts/profiles/progress/results/tournaments.
- Public WebSockets: 60Hz simulation, 20Hz batched inputs and snapshots, client movement/startup prediction/reconciliation, remote interpolation. Serialize all runtime needed to restore ability state, air resources, moving platforms, deployables and randomness. Server alone decides hits/results. Private P2P stays explicit, never silently substitutes public server authority.
- Public lobby shows online pool/open activities/Quick Play; one compatible preference pool for stock duel/FFA/2v2/Royale. Timed/Hill custom first. Preserve parties; public teams max two per party, larger local groups use FFA/Royale/private. No silent public bot fill, ranked MMR, text/voice chat.
- 15-second reconnect, neutral disconnected inputs, eventual forfeit without stopping others. Public pause is local only. Idempotent results; checkpoint recovery or honest interrupted result on server restart.
- Cloudflare Free only: initial four concurrent public matches, 64 lobby connections, 1200 human player-minutes/day; reserve before admission and show queue/capacity/reset. Measure/adjust down if needed; no paid upgrades. Idle services hibernate. A South America hint currently maps to Eastern North America, so Argentina latency and server feasibility must be measured early, not promised.
- Guests immediate; optional Google/GitHub/passkey through Better Auth with Drizzle/D1. Social signup/link, passkey enrollment/login, linked provider recovery. Explicit authenticated provider linking. Cosmetic/achievement sync and verified online stats distinguished from local practice. Couch guest identities are separate, not all credited to primary browser account.
- Elimination: 4/8/16 duel entrants or 4/8 teams, best-of-three. Round robin: 4–8 entrants/teams, best-of-one. Wins/head-to-head/KO differential; unresolved ties share rank. Public/private entry, persistent progress, bounded spectators; shared-browser entrants not scheduled into simultaneous matches; only authoritative results advance. Royale is standalone, no Royale league scoring.
- Protocol/content v2 adds ruleset/mode/authority/party/seat readiness and full state. Auth/profile/lobby/match/tournament APIs, existing private routes remain. Saved preferences migrate; old rooms drain and incompatible clients receive refresh action.
